# My Favorite Series: Suits

## About the Series

Suits is a legal drama series about lawyers working in a law firm in New York. The story mainly focuses on Harvey Specter and Mike Ross. Mike is very smart and has a photographic memory, but he doesn't have a law degree. Despite that, Harvey gives him a chance to work as his associate.

## My Favorite Characters

- **Harvey Specter** - I can see myself in him I like how confident he is and how he handles difficult situations.
- **Mike Ross** - He is intelligent and has a photographic memory.
- **Donna Paulsen** - She is smart and always knows what's happening around the office.
- **Louis Litt** - He can be funny sometimes, but he is also good at his job.

## Why I Like This Series

I like Suits because I enjoy watching how they handle cases and solve problems. I also like the conversations between the characters, especially Harvey's comebacks. The series is entertaining and sometimes teaches you about confidence, decision-making, and how to deal with different people.

## My Rating

**10/10**

I would recommend this series to anyone who enjoys drama, business, and interesting conversations.
